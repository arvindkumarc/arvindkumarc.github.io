# Projects

A selection of projects I've worked on throughout my career.

---

#### Crypto based gaming platform

Gaming platforms using Token Bonding Curve approach to adjust the price of Athletes within the platform, helping users who own these tokens to have demand and performance-based value.

*Tech: NodeJS, Firebase, React Native, Solana Blockchain*

---

#### AI driven Business Intelligence - Finance and Retail

Retail businesses with time-consuming monotonous tasks automated with intelligence, saving huge amounts of time and delivering value-based returns. The platform was also adapted to predict cash replenishment at ATM Kiosk machines.

*Tech: R, Python, Java, Go, Kotlin, PostgreSQL, Spring Boot, Flask, ReactJS, Docker, Prometheus*

---

#### Targeted Advertisements Platform

A leading shopping mall in India needed a platform to correlate physical and virtual consumer experiences. Imagine liking an item online and getting a notification when you walk into their mall where the product is available.

*Tech: Java, Coffeescript, AWS Kinesis, Apache Storm, BLE beacon integration*

---

#### Movie Ticket Aggregator

Aggregation of tier 2 town-based theatres in India into a simple application to book tickets online. The challenge was working with Remote box office setup (Vista), keeping it realtime to block seats for online users.

*Tech: Golang, PostgreSQL, LocustIO*

---

#### Universal Order Management System

An antibody producer and distributor upgrading their platform from legacy to a completely rewritten system. Worked on the Order Management system handling delivery timelines across the globe.

*Tech: C#.Net, MSSQL Server*

---

#### K12 Education Suite

Tablet-based education platform for K12 kids. Realtime integration with device (BYOD or Managed), classroom presence integration, subject and session management, homework sharing, and grading system.

*Tech: Ruby On Rails, XMPP, Android, Gatling, MySQL, Cucumber*

---

#### Helpline for Intensive Care - Ghana, Africa

Pre and post-natal care solution for hilly regions in Ghana. Daily calls to provide advice on what to expect, with support nurses knowing whom to visit in emergencies. A collaboration between ThoughtWorks, Bill-Melinda Gates Foundation, and Grameen Foundation.

*Tech: Java, ActiveMQ, Asterisk, MySQL, Spring*

---

#### Element Management System

High-performance monitoring solution for a switch manufacturer. Worked on the performance management module with a pluggable architecture supporting any device and module dynamically.

*Tech: J2EE, JBoss, MObjects, JMS, MySQL*

---

#### Hospital Management System

Complete solution for TLM hospitals across India to manage Patient Records, Pharmacy, and Doctor visits.

*Tech: Java, Spring, Hibernate, Javascript, jQuery*
